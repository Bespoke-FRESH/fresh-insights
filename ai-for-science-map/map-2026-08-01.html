Stage assignments are our coding, not the companies’ — the average entry is tagged into almost half the lifecycle, so treat the stage counts as a vocabulary rather than a measurement. 32 of 60 sources carry no date, and most are organizations describing themselves, which is the right source for what a product claims to do and the wrong one for whether it works. Market structure

What is changing

The field is moving from general-purpose scientific chat toward specialized, executable, institutionally embedded systems.

Execution is becoming a product layer

Research agents are increasingly connecting literature, hypothesis generation, computational tools, CROs, and automated laboratories rather than stopping at text generation.

Domain stacks are separating from general agents

Chemistry, materials, biomedicine, evidence synthesis, and publication integrity are developing distinct products with specialized data, tools, and validation requirements.

Scientific memory is moving inside institutions

Enterprise platforms are combining internal experiments, documents, knowledge graphs, and external evidence to support organizational rather than individual workflows.

Trust infrastructure remains fragmented

Evidence grading, reproducibility checks, image integrity, statistical verification, peer review, and regulatory translation still sit in separate systems.

Unmet needs

Strategic gap analysis

The largest white spaces sit between workflow layers rather than inside any single point solution.

End-to-end provenance

Few systems preserve a machine-readable chain from source claim through analysis, experiment, manuscript, review, and translation.

Open opportunityA claim graph with immutable source/version IDs, transformation logs, and decision lineage.

Independent scientific-agent benchmarks

Many comparisons are vendor-run, use visible platform identity, static tasks, or LLM judges.

Open opportunityHidden fresh tasks, executable environments, preregistered scoring, blinded domain experts, and replication.

Protocol-to-publication consistency

Registration, protocol, analysis plan, manuscript, and supplement are rarely compared automatically.

Open opportunityA deterministic + model-assisted discrepancy engine with severity and provenance.

Evidence-grade calibration

Displaying a grade is not the same as demonstrating agreement, stability, calibration, or better decisions.

Open opportunityExternal GRADE-trained adjudication, repeated-question stability tests, and decision-impact studies.

Lab interoperability

Agents, CROs, robotic labs, ELNs, and instruments lack shared execution/provenance schemas.

Open opportunityOpen protocol, constraint, QC, result, and failure-return standards.

Adversarial peer-review evaluation

AI reviewers can be manipulated by presentation, laundering, prompt injection, prestige, and correlated judgments.

Open opportunityRolling contamination-resistant benchmarks and bounded deployment standards.

Outcome evidence

Speed, citations, and generated artifacts are intermediate outcomes.

Open opportunityProspective studies of reproducibility, candidate quality, clinical/industrial outcomes, and total cost.
