## Claude Code

- **Publishing is a Class-C, ask-first action.** Rendering locally and showing Josh the result is the
  default; `git push` to `main` is not something to do on your own judgment.
- Use `fresh-weekly-essay` for drafting, `citation-verification` before showing a draft, and
  `artifact-share` for interactive artifacts.
- Consider running the `claim-auditor` subagent over any draft before Josh reads it — this is the
  surface where an unverified claim becomes public under his byline.
